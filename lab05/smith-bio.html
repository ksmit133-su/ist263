Kay Smith's Biography ™

Architecture student. Amateur trail menace. Unofficial murder mystery consultant.

Personal info

My name is Kay Smith, and I'm an architecture major who got hooked on the field the way most obsessions
start, by staring at other people's homes a little too long.Something about watching a home get customized and renovated to
fit the actual humans living in it not some catalog version of them pulled me in, and I never really left.

Four years after being in school, I've built up a small arsenal of technical skills: Rhino, Revit, and ArchiCAD for 3D modeling,
plus Illustrator, Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and InDesign for making things look intentional instead of accidental. I also know my
way around Microsoft Office and can do basic structural analysis in Excel, which is a sentence I never thought I'd be proud of,
and yet here I am!

Outside of school, I'm an avid hiker and sketch artist, which mostly means I drag a sketchbook up mountains and blame "the light"
when I stop to rest. Most weekends you'll find me outdoors with family or friends, trying to capture a view before my hands get
too cold to hold a pencil. When I'm not doing that, I'm swimming, playing tennis, or on the couch with a murder mystery book or
show, I don't discriminate, I just need someone to have died under mysterious circumstances by episode two.
