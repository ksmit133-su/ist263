About me

KS

I’m a Bachelor of Architecture candidate at Syracuse University, graduating in May 2027, with experience in architectural offices and residential construction. I work in Revit, ArchiCAD and Rhino and enjoy full-scale fabrication: I designed and built a greenhouse from concept to completion. I’m interested in residential, adaptive reuse and community projects, and I’m looking for a Syracuse-based design role.

I came to architecture through a love of customizing and renovating homes. That interest in how people live in and change their houses still shapes my work, and I care more about how space and materials come together than about appearance alone.

I’m drawn to community-driven, socially engaged design, including refugee shelter, domestic space and landscape architecture. I look for projects where the people who will use a place help shape it.

What I’m interested in

  • Residential design and adaptive reuse
  • Community and participatory design-build projects
  • Refugee shelter, especially woven and textile-informed approaches
  • Gardens and green space as social infrastructure, from private yard to shared ground
  • Thresholds between inside and outside, such as porches and greenhouses

Designers and projects that influence me

Abeer Seikaly, Shigeru Ban, Nader Khalili, Jan Gehl, the Bidi Bidi Performing Arts Centre, the Granby Four Streets project and the Vauban district.
