JOURNALS ABOUT

Hi, I'm Kay! 👋

I’m a senior studying architecture at Syracuse University, and someone who loves documenting the design process one sketchbook at a time.

I came to architecture through customizing and renovating homes, and I keep returning to community-driven design: shelter, domestic space, and landscape.

Over the past five years, I’ve filled notebooks with floor plans, section cuts, site studies, and quick travel sketches. Some drawings took hours, and others were scribbled right before studio pin-up—but every page reminds me of a different phase of learning how spaces come together.

I wanted to give all these physical notebooks a digital home where you can pick one off the shelf, flip through the spreads, and see where the thinking happens.

Hope you enjoy exploring the archive! ♡
