Personal info

My name is Kay Smith, and I am an architecture major drawn to the field through my fascination with how residential homes are customized and renovated to reflect the people who inhabit them.
Over the past four years, I have developed strong technical proficiency in 3D modeling through Rhino, Revit, and ArchiCAD, alongside representational skills in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop,
Premiere Pro, and InDesign. I also have a working knowledge of Microsoft Office and basic structural analysis in Excel. Outside of academics, I am an avid hiker and sketch artist, often
heading outdoors with family and friends most weekends to capture what I see. I also enjoy swimming and tennis and unwind with murder mystery books and shows.
